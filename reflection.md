# Personal reflection — write this yourself, without AI

The assignment asks for a half-page reflection written by the candidate. This file is intentionally left for your own account.

Please address:
- What you got wrong at the start.
- What you would cut from the tool if you had to simplify it.
- Where AI helped you.
- Where AI wasted time or made an assumption you had to correct.
- What changed after speaking to the person and collecting real data.

Do not paste a generic reflection. Be specific about your actual visit, decisions and experience.
