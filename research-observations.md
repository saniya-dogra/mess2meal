# 20 research-based findings — Mess2Meal

> **Evidence type: SECONDARY RESEARCH, NOT FIELD OBSERVATIONS.**
>
> These 20 entries are findings extracted or carefully summarised from published reports and Government of India replies. They are useful research evidence for the assignment's background section, but they do **not** count as 20 observations made by the student during a local visit. They contain no invented visit dates, locations, people, quantities measured by the student or interview quotes.

**Research review date:** 22 November 2026  
**Geographic scope:** India and global context  
**Primary sources:** UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024; Government of India parliamentary replies; MoFPI/NABCONS and ICAR-CIPHET estimates; FAO and UNEP food loss and waste resources.

## Twenty findings from published research

| # | Research finding | Scope / what it means for Mess2Meal | Source |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | UNEP's 2024 estimate for household food waste in India is **55 kg per person per year** for 2022. | Establishes a national household-level baseline; it is not a local mess or restaurant measurement. | [Government of India, Lok Sabha reply, 12 Mar 2025](https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/PMS/writereaddata/2025_LS_B_2181.pdf) |
| 2 | The global household food-waste average cited in the same reply is **79 kg per person per year**. | Provides context for interpreting India's household estimate. | [Government of India, Lok Sabha reply, 12 Mar 2025](https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/PMS/writereaddata/2025_LS_B_2181.pdf) |
| 3 | UNEP's 2024 report estimates that **1.05 billion tonnes** of food were wasted globally at retail, food-service and household levels in 2022. | Shows why measurement must include multiple stages, not just farms. | [UNEP Food Waste Index 2024 — key messages](https://www.fao.org/platform-food-loss-waste/flw-events/international-day-food-loss-and-waste/food-waste-index-report-2024/ar) |
| 4 | The global distribution of consumer-level food waste in the UNEP summary is approximately **60% household, 28% food service and 12% retail**. | Food service is a distinct measurement category relevant to messes, restaurants and cafeterias. | [UNEP Food Waste Index 2024 — key messages](https://www.fao.org/platform-food-loss-waste/flw-events/international-day-food-loss-and-waste/food-waste-index-report-2024/ar) |
| 5 | FAO summarises global food loss after harvest and before retail at **13.2%**. | Food loss upstream is different from surplus cooked food at a mess; the project should keep categories separate. | [FAO — Food Loss and Food Waste](https://www.fao.org/policy-support/policy-themes/food-loss-and-food-waste/) |
| 6 | FAO summarises a further **19%** of food as wasted at retail, food-service and household levels, based on 2024 UNEP statistics. | Supports measuring food service separately from post-harvest loss. | [FAO — Food Loss and Food Waste](https://www.fao.org/policy-support/policy-themes/food-loss-and-food-waste/) |
| 7 | FAO reports food loss and waste are associated with an estimated **8–10% of global greenhouse-gas emissions**. | Gives environmental context, but Mess2Meal must not claim emissions savings without a defensible calculation. | [FAO — Food Loss and Food Waste](https://www.fao.org/policy-support/policy-themes/food-loss-and-food-waste/) |
| 8 | UNEP's 2024 report expands guidance for measuring waste in **retail, food service and household** sectors and discusses methods and limitations. | A Mess2Meal field dataset should state its measurement method and uncertainty. | [UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024](https://wedocs.unep.org/items/dbe2cd4c-8384-4636-8359-5847f42b9711) |
| 9 | WRI India notes that the India household estimate draws on **seven sub-national studies from 2014–2018**. | The national estimate has an underlying evidence limitation; it should not be presented as a fresh local measurement. | [WRI India, 27 Sep 2024](https://wri-india.org/perspectives/defining-food-waste-sustainable-food-systems) |
| 10 | WRI India notes that those underlying studies had small samples, different durations and varying estimation methods. | Reinforces the need to record how each local quantity is weighed, counted or estimated. | [WRI India, 27 Sep 2024](https://wri-india.org/perspectives/defining-food-waste-sustainable-food-systems) |
| 11 | WRI India identifies a lack of consistent food-waste data for food services and retail in India. | A small, carefully documented local food-service dataset could help describe one site, without claiming national representativeness. | [WRI India, 27 Sep 2024](https://wri-india.org/perspectives/defining-food-waste-sustainable-food-systems) |
| 12 | A 2025 Lok Sabha reply states that **82 agencies** were registered under IFSA and the network reached **90+ cities** at the time of that reply. | India already has a food-recovery network; Mess2Meal should complement, not claim to replace, it. | [Government of India, Lok Sabha reply, 12 Mar 2025](https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/PMS/writereaddata/2025_LS_B_2181.pdf) |
| 13 | The same reply describes FSSAI's **Save Food Share Food** initiative as promoting surplus-food donation, including by food businesses and caterers. | The donor workflow should record source and responsible contact, and direct users to existing networks where appropriate. | [Government of India, Lok Sabha reply, 12 Mar 2025](https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/PMS/writereaddata/2025_LS_B_2181.pdf) |
| 14 | India's Food Safety and Standards (Recovery and Distribution of Surplus Food) Regulations were notified in **2019**. | Surplus redistribution has a food-safety regulatory context; the prototype cannot itself certify compliance. | [FSSAI regulation PDF](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf) |
| 15 | The FSSAI regulations prohibit distribution of unsafe food. | Matching must never be treated as a safety decision; a responsible person must assess food before donation. | [FSSAI regulation PDF](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf) |
| 16 | The regulations require surplus to be handed over with enough time for it to be consumed within its shelf life. | Preparation and best-before/use-by details are operationally important fields, but must be supplied accurately by the donor. | [FSSAI regulation PDF](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf) |
| 17 | The regulations' schedule addresses segregation and suitable packing to prevent contamination. | The app can capture reported packaging/handling details, but cannot inspect them remotely. | [FSSAI regulation PDF](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf) |
| 18 | The regulations address hygienic storage at suitable temperatures and keeping surplus away from waste material. | A free-text storage field is only a record; it is not proof that temperature or hygiene requirements were met. | [FSSAI regulation PDF](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf) |
| 19 | A 2025 Rajya Sabha reply reports NABCONS 2022 post-harvest loss ranges of **3.89–5.92% for cereals** and **5.65–6.74% for pulses**. | These are agricultural post-harvest estimates, not cooked-food surplus measurements; keep them separate in the report. | [Government of India, Rajya Sabha reply, 29 Jul 2025](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/annex/268/AS93_kW1Yhn.pdf?source=pqars) |
| 20 | The same reply reports NABCONS 2022 post-harvest loss ranges of **6.02–15.05% for fruits** and **4.87–11.61% for vegetables**. | Shows that loss varies by commodity; it should not be converted into an assumed quantity of edible surplus at a local outlet. | [Government of India, Rajya Sabha reply, 29 Jul 2025](https://www.sansad.in/getFile/annex/268/AS93_kW1Yhn.pdf?source=pqars) |

## How to use these findings

1. Cite them in the **secondary research / background** section of the report.
2. Use them to justify why the prototype records food type, quantity, time, handling notes and handover status.
3. Keep national household estimates, agricultural post-harvest loss and food-service waste as separate categories.
4. Do not put these rows into `data/fieldwork-template.csv` or label them `FIELD_OBSERVED`.
5. The assignment's 20 primary observations still require a real local visit. Record actual items, dates, times, quantities and measurement methods in the fieldwork CSV.

## Limitations

- These are published findings at national or global scale, not observations made at the student's chosen site.
- The sources use different years, sectors and methodologies; their numbers are not directly interchangeable.
- The 2025 parliamentary replies repeat estimates from earlier studies/reports; the reply date is not the measurement year.
- No conclusion is made here about current food surplus in any specific college mess, restaurant, hostel or neighbourhood.
