## 2. Observation plan
- Identify leftover untouched surplus separately from plate waste; they are not the same.
- Ask whether quantities are weighed or estimated. Record the method.
- Note preparation time and best-before/last-consumption information only when staff can verify it.
- Observe storage, packaging and collection arrangements without handling food unless authorised.
- Note who decides whether food can be shared and who arranges collection.
- Do not photograph people, documents, phone numbers or private areas without explicit permission.
