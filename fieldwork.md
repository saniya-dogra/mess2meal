## 5. Operational workflow

1. Donor identifies possible surplus.
2. Donor records source, food, quantity, preparation time, best-before information, storage description and pickup location.
3. Coordinator reviews completeness and suitability.
4. The system filters and ranks possible recipients by activity, category, capacity, urgency and entered distance.
5. Recipient confirms acceptance.
6. Parties arrange collection.
7. Physical handover is confirmed.
8. Coordinator records the final status.

A match suggestion is not acceptance. Acceptance is not collection. Only a verified handover should be counted as delivered.
